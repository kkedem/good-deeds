# Good Deeds – Personas

> **Scope note.** These personas describe the full product vision and go beyond the hackathon demo's
> scope (see PRD.md Section 3.2). Maya's streaks, badges, favourites and partner rewards, and Sam's
> repeat-volunteer network, are **out of scope for this build**. Sam's charity affiliation itself is
> fine to keep as context — for the demo she signs up and posts exactly like Dave, as a normal
> community poster. What's cut is the organisation **verification** step/badge, not the charity
> framing.

## Meet Maya, the Social Doer
**Maya, 35 | London | Marketing Manager**

**"I want to do something worthwhile, stay active and meet people, but it needs to fit around my life."**

### About Maya

- Fitness-focused and regularly runs, hikes and attends classes
- Active on Instagram and Strava
- Wants to contribute locally but doesn't know where to start
- Enjoys meeting new people and volunteering with friends
- Motivated by visible progress, challenges and recognition

### Her Good Deeds journey

**1. Personalise**
Signs up → sets her location → creates a profile → selects **outdoor, active and social** preferences

**2. Build trust**
Verifies her ID → chooses whether she wants to volunteer **solo, with friends or as part of a group**

**3. Discover**
Gets personalised opportunities → filters between **active and less-active roles** → saves favourites

**4. Do good**
Joins a community clean-up → marks the deed complete → sees her **hours and impact recorded**

**5. Share & return**
Shares her achievement on social media → builds a participation streak → unlocks **badges and partner rewards**

### What success looks like

**1 deed → 4 deeds/month → 12-month streak**

Track **deeds completed • volunteer hours • causes supported • streak length • social shares • rewards unlocked**

### Her motivation

**Fitness + community + social connection + visible impact + recognition**

**Pitch-deck takeaway:** Good Deeds turns Maya's intention to help into a social, rewarding and repeatable habit.

## Meet Sam, the Local Connector
**Sam, 45 | Part-time Foodbank Volunteer Coordinator | Local Charity**

**"People want to help, but reaching them at the right time and getting them to commit is really difficult."**

### About Sam

- Works part-time coordinating volunteers for a locally run foodbank that is part of a charity
- Primarily needs volunteers to help distribute food within the local community
- Regularly needs to fill last-minute gaps when there aren't enough volunteers
- Currently reaches people through flyers, Facebook and word of mouth
- Often gets initial interest, but struggles to turn that interest into firm commitments
- Has lower digital confidence, but is reasonably comfortable using everyday apps on their phone
- Wants a simple way to reach more local people at exactly the time help is needed

### Their Good Deeds journey

**1. Create**
Opens Good Deeds → chooses **Post an opportunity** → adds what help is needed, when, where and how many volunteers are required

**2. Reach**
Publishes the opportunity → Good Deeds makes it discoverable to people looking for local volunteering opportunities, rather than Sam relying solely on flyers, Facebook and word of mouth

**3. Fill the gaps**
Sees who has committed → identifies any remaining spaces → promotes the opportunity to people who could help at the right time

**4. Deliver**
Volunteers arrive → help distribute food locally → Sam can see who participated and mark the opportunity as complete

**5. Build a volunteer network**
Completed volunteers become part of a growing pool of people who can discover and participate in future opportunities, reducing the effort required to repeatedly find help from scratch

### What success looks like

**Last-minute gap → opportunity posted → local people reached → volunteers committed → shift filled**

Track **opportunities posted • volunteer spaces filled • confirmed volunteers • attendance • unfilled spaces • repeat volunteers**

### Their motivation

**Support the community + distribute food to people who need it + reach more volunteers + fill last-minute gaps + spend less time chasing people**

### Their biggest pain point

**Interest isn't the same as commitment.** Sam can already advertise through Facebook, flyers and word of mouth. The real problem is reaching people when they're available and giving them an easy way to make a firm commitment.

**Pitch-deck takeaway:** Good Deeds helps Sam turn local goodwill into dependable volunteers, making it quicker and easier to fill gaps and get help where it's needed.
